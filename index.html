WHAT AUDIOX DOES

Your sounds. Your panel.

AudioX is focused on making custom sound management simple instead of filling the menu with features you never use.

♫

Custom sound folder

Keep your supported sound files inside one sounds folder so your AudioX library stays easy to manage.

◈

In-game sound panel

Pick sounds from your AudioX library and use them through the menu while playing, when the installed build supports playback.

⌁

VR-first controls

Large readable controls, clean spacing, quick selection, and motion designed around Gorilla Tag movement.

SOUND LIBRARY

Drop in your sounds.

Choose your AudioX sounds folder to preview supported files in the browser. The actual in-game playback is handled by the AudioX build.

HOW IT WORKS

Built around your library.

01

Add a sounds folder

Store the audio you want to use in one dedicated folder and load it into AudioX.

02

Pick a sound

AudioX shows your files as a clean list with playback controls so you can quickly find the sound you want.

03

Play through the menu

The AudioX mod is designed to connect your custom library to an in-game sound panel for playback while playing.
